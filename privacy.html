Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Home/Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Tongue Feast (operated by Siddhivinayak Products, MIDC Chikalthana, Chh. Sambhaji Nagar, Maharashtra, India) collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data. We are committed to prioritizing your privacy and operating in compliance with applicable Indian regulations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect specifically targeted information necessary for our business operations, primarily when you submit an inquiry through our Contact Form. The exact data points collected include:

  • Identity Data: Full Name.
  • Contact Information: Phone Number and Email Address.
  • Location Data: City/Location.
  • Business Context: Selected Enquiry Type (Wholesale, Distributor, Horeca, White Labeling, Export).
  • Inquiry Content: Free-text messages related to product quantities, distribution, or general questions.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The information we collect is strictly utilized for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To process, validate, and respond to commercial and consumer inquiries.
  • To facilitate communication regarding bulk orders, distributor partnerships, and export opportunities.
  • To send administrative communication related to our ongoing business relationship.
  • To comply with regulatory and legal obligations.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We maintain a strict data retention policy. Personal data submitted via inquiries will be retained for a period of one (1) year from the date of the last interaction, after which it is securely deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by law or necessary for ongoing contractual obligations.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Analytics

To improve our website’s functionality, user experience, and targeted marketing, we utilize the following third-party services which may deploy cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics: Used to understand traffic patterns and user behavior.
  • Meta Pixel: Used to measure advertising effectiveness and structure relevant campaigns.
  • Hosting/Infrastructure Providers: Necessary session cookies ensuring the website functions securely and performantly.

You may configure your browser to decline non-essential cookies. However, restricting necessary cookies may impact the technical performance of our website.

5. WhatsApp and Third-Party Platforms

When you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, your phone number and conversation data will be processed through WhatsApp (a Meta company). This communication is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy. We utilize this platform strictly to facilitate immediate customer service and business inquiries.

6. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal data. Data is shared exclusively on a need-to-know basis with trusted service providers essential for business operations. We deploy appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to defend against unauthorized access or breaches. Despite these measures, absolute security over internet communications cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your Rights and Consent Withdrawal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you retain control over your personal data. You possess the right to:

  • Access the personal data we currently hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erase your personal data from our systems.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may revoke your consent for data processing at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer. Upon verification, we will cease processing your data within statutory timelines.
